The customer's proposal: a PDF made from the build sheet. Once signed, with its terms, it is the contract.

- Open it with the build sheet's **Proposal** button (it saves the build sheet first). The page lists what to fix **Before sending**, then shows the PDF as the customer gets it. **Download PDF** saves a copy.
- **Before sending** catches things like a production estimate that is missing or out of date, savings inputs still needed, no salesperson, a passed valid-until date, panels outside the saved proposal view, or no Price to Compare.
- The proposal number (year plus 4 digits) is given the first time it opens. A copied build sheet gets its own number.
- Internal cost, markup, margin and commission never print.

## Where each part comes from

- **The build sheet:** Proposal section (date, valid until, addressed-to contact, which production model leads, customer notes, financing estimate), Savings section (assumptions and incentives), Line items, Arrays, Warranty summary and Payment schedule.
- **The opportunity:** customer, site and the utility bill.
- **The site layout:** the aerial on the System Summary page, framed by the saved proposal view.
- **Sales rates:** company block, cover letter, next steps and savings defaults.
- **Proposal terms:** Clarifications and Terms & Conditions. The footer names the terms version it printed with.

The production guarantee is turned on per build sheet by an admin only.

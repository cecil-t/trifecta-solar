Everything about one job: details, steps, notes and its log.

## Top of the page

- Phase and status chips, then the Drive folder, **Print summary** (one page for the designer, to print or save as PDF; no steps or dates) and **Edit**.
- **Notify customer** (red card) appears when a step with a customer-update rule happens. Tell the customer, then click **Customer notified**, or **Not notified** to clear it without telling them. Steps with a rule carry a red dot.
- **Cards:** Customer, Jurisdiction (flags a municipality the address lookup disagrees with), System, Contract (with the sales commission), Notes (one shared block; a save never overwrites someone's newer save), Tasks linked to this project, and the latest comment.

## Steps

Grouped by phase: Pre-Install, Installation, Closeout and Payments. Every field saves as soon as you change it.

- **Needed:** Yes / No / ? for question steps. Other steps are needed unless marked Not needed.
- **Completed:** the date it happened. The check mark fills in today; the x clears it.
- **Target:** a planned date. Light blue when set, cream once it passes, red 30+ days past.
- **Ref #** (permit, work order and similar), **Asgn** (owner; feeds their My open items) and **Note**.
- The **...** menu: Not needed, Rename, Duplicate, Add sub-task, Remove.
- Badges: **clock** starts the day count, **prereq** counts toward Clear to install, **phase** moves the project to its next phase.
- A check mark beside a phase's name means every step in it is resolved.
- Add a custom step at the bottom of a phase, or bring one in with Add from template.

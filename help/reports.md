Company numbers, each report on its own page with its own filters. Every report prints on its own (Print button). They are easiest to read on a computer.

- **Sales:** projects, contract value and kW by salesperson, quarter and month.
- **Projection:** the next 12 months at the pace of the last quarter, this year, and the rolling 12 and 24 months.
- **Project time:** days from contract signed to PTO, plus a list of jobs in progress. Untick **Count** on a job to leave an outlier out of the average.
- **Service:** tickets, trips, man-hours and amounts billed, by coverage and month.
- **Opportunities** (salespeople and admins): the sales pipeline by salesperson, win rate, pricing, every contingency and price override with its reason, quotes going cold (nothing done in 30 days) and lost reasons. Salespeople see only their own.
- **Sales commissions** (when you have access): opens the commissions list.

The Reports page also has **At a glance** counts for projects, service tickets, tasks and lists, and the latest activity.

Your starting point: what needs you today, and the company at a glance.

- **Weather:** the 5-day forecast for the office area.
- **Projects and Service tiles:** counts by phase. Click one to open that list. Ready to invoice turns orange when tickets are waiting to be billed.
- **Monitor tiles:** production across every monitored system, yesterday and the last 7 days.
- **Notify customer** (only when something is waiting): updates to send customers, by project. Lines in bold are yours. Clear them on the project page once the customer is told.
- **My tasks:** to-dos assigned to you.
- **My open items:** project steps assigned to you that are actionable now: the project has reached that phase, the target date is within 30 days, or it is the next payment. Steps from a phase the project has moved past are left out. Click a project row to show its items, and an item to jump to it on the project page.
- **Latest activity:** recent changes and comments across the tracker.

## Tip

Open items come from the **Asgn** (owner) column on project steps. Steps with nobody assigned never show up here for anyone.

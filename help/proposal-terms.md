The fixed text on every proposal: the opening, Clarifications, Terms & Conditions, Final Authorizations and any addendum. Everyone can read it; admins edit it.

- The text is kept as **blocks**. Each block prints only when its condition holds for the build sheet (in-house or SolarInsure warranty, production guarantee, battery, residential or not), so one set covers every kind of job.
- Placeholders in braces (the proposal number, the insurance sentence and others) are filled from the build sheet and Sales rates. The proposal warns before sending if one can't be filled.
- **Save as a new version** keeps the old one. Add a note on what changed. Every proposal's footer names the terms version it printed with.
- **How blocks work**, on the page, lists the conditions and placeholders.
- Company details, the cover letter and next steps are on Sales rates, not here.

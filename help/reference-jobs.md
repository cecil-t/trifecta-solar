Built, monitored systems with their signed proposal's production estimate, used to check the production models against reality. Admins only.

- Each run takes the site's typical-year weather and runs SAM's detailed model and PVWatts v8 on the job's equipment and arrays, with the losses the proposal stated.
- The results compare the proposal, both models and the monitored production month by month.
- **Summary:** monitored production as a percent of each estimate over the last 12 complete months. The gray figure leaves out December to February, since snow on the panels is in none of the estimates.
- **Run every job**, or run one at a time. Jobs are added on the server with an import file.

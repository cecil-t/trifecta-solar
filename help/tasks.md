One-off to-dos assigned to a person, separate from project steps.

- **Assign a task:** what needs to be done, who it's for, an optional due date, an optional link to a project or service ticket, and details.
- **Tabs:** My tasks, Assigned by me, All open, Completed and All. The person filter narrows All open, Completed and All.
- Click the box beside a task to mark it completed; click it again to reopen it.
- Due dates show **Due today** and **Overdue** in color.
- A task's own page has its details and comments.
- Tasks linked to a project or ticket also show in that record's Tasks card, which has its own **+ Assign a task** button that links the new task for you.

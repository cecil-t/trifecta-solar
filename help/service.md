Service tickets for systems already in the field, whether Trifecta installed them or not.

## The list

**Open** (including tickets with a visit scheduled), **Ready to invoice**, **Completed** and **All**. Completed folds earlier years under a header.

## A ticket

- **Request:** where it came from, the owner and the problem. A ticket started from the Monitor page's Open ticket comes filled in (alert, system size, portal link, and the customer and project it matches).
- **Customer and site:** pick the customer or add one. Link the original project when Trifecta installed it.
- **Monitoring system:** link the system from the Monitor page; a blank portal link fills in from it.
- **Coverage:** Billable, Warranty or SolarInsure. Billable and SolarInsure tickets need an invoice; Warranty does not.
- **Visits:** date, who went, what was done, trips and man-hours. The totals show under Billing and on the Service report.
- **Next visit scheduled** marks the ticket Scheduled.
- **Mark completed** with its date. Billable and SolarInsure tickets then wait under Ready to invoice until **Mark invoiced** (amount, invoice number and date).
- Tasks and the log work as they do on projects.

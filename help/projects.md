Every installation job, from contract signed to closeout.

## The list

- **Tabs:** Active (Pre-Install, Installation and Closeout together), each of those phases, On hold, Completed, Cancelled and All. **Notify customer** shows up when customer updates are waiting.
- Completed and Cancelled fold earlier years under a header; click it to open.
- **Status** shows the phase, plus On hold and Clear to install. **Days** counts from contract signed to PTO, or to today until PTO.
- The salesperson filter is remembered for you. A search looks across every project.

## How a project moves through its phases

The phase follows the project's steps; there is no phase to set by hand.

- **Pre-Install** until the step that marks the install started is completed, then **Installation**.
- **Closeout** once PTO is completed, and **Completed** once every Closeout and Payments step is resolved.
- **Clear to install** shows in Pre-Install once the required steps (building permit, interconnection approval) are in.
- **On hold** and **Cancelled** are set on the project's Edit page with a reason. Mark completed there finishes a project by hand.

## New project

Fill it in from the contract: customer (or add one), site address (the township and county are looked up as you type), system equipment, contract price and funding. Its steps are copied from the Task template.

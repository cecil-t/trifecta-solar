Quote requests from the company website's form, and Claude, which reads electric bills. Admins only.

## Website quote form

- New requests are checked every 15 minutes on weekdays from 8 AM to 6 PM, and once at noon on Saturday and Sunday.
- Each request shows as **Pending** at the top of [Sales](/presales) until someone creates an opportunity from it or dismisses it.
- **Check the form** lists the form's fields (and which ones the tracker uses) and its newest entries. Nothing is saved.
- **Check the website now** brings in new requests right away, like a scheduled check.

## Claude

- Reads uploaded bills: on a website request, and with **Read a bill** on the opportunity form. The bill is sent to Claude and not kept here.
- **Model** sets which Claude model reads bills. **Test a bill** shows everything it found and what the read cost, without saving anything.
- Every call is counted with its cost, shown here for the last 30 days and all time.
- Keys live in the server's settings file, not in the tracker. "Not set up" means a key is missing there.

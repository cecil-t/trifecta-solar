The tracker holds Trifecta's sales, projects, service and to-dos in one place. Google Drive stays the file store; projects and tickets link to their Drive folders.

## The top bar

- **Main menu:** Dashboard, Sales (salespeople and admins), Projects, Service, Tasks, Reports and Monitor.
- **Search** finds projects and service tickets together: name, customer, number, municipality, a ticket's problem or address. A single match opens it directly.
- **Lists:** customers, municipalities, the directory of third parties and the sales lists. Admins also see the Task template, Users and the API pages.
- **Your initials:** My account, Help, About and Sign out.

## Everywhere

- The **?** beside a page's title opens help for that page. Every topic is under your initials > Help.
- Every change to a project, ticket, task or opportunity is logged with who and when, at the bottom of its page.
- In a comment box on a computer, Enter posts and Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- Searching on Projects, Service or Tasks always looks across everything (the All tab).
- Words used the same way everywhere: **Completed** means finished; **Tasks** are one-off to-dos assigned to a person; **Open items** are project steps that need attention now.
- On a phone, use the browser's "Add to Home screen" and the tracker opens like an app. You stay signed in on each device until you sign it out.

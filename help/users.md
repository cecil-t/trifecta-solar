Staff sign-ins. Admins only.

- **Add user:** full name, email (used to sign in), initials and an optional phone (printed on proposals for salespeople). Set a password and pass it on; they can change it under My account.
- **Admin:** manages users, the task template, the directory, municipality providers, Sales rates and Proposal terms, and sees every opportunity and commission.
- **Sales:** sees their own Sales opportunities and edits the Sales catalog.
- **Active:** can sign in. Turn it off when someone leaves; their name and history stay on everything they touched.
- **Devices:** where they are signed in. Sign out all devices if a phone or laptop is lost.
- Each user's page shows their activity.

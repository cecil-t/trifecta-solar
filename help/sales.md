Jobs being quoted, before they become projects. A salesperson sees the opportunities they are the salesperson on or created; admins see everyone's.

## Opportunities

- An opportunity is one customer and one site being quoted. A repeat customer gets a new opportunity for each new site or expansion.
- **New opportunity:** the customer (pick one or add one), salesperson, lead source, site address and the utility bill. Saving opens its first build sheet.
- **Tabs:** Open, Sold and Lost. **Prospect** marks a customer with no projects yet.

## The opportunity page

- **Build sheets:** each card is one design with its price, size and margin. Make as many as the customer needs; a red or orange chip means its catalog prices need a look.
- **Edit opportunity:** customer, site, lead and the utility bill. The bill's yearly use, usage profile, rate from the bill and Price to Compare drive the savings on the proposal, so fill them in before sending one.
- **Mark sold:** pick the build sheet that sold. **Mark lost:** an optional reason, which shows on the Opportunities report. **Reopen** undoes either.
- **Log:** notes and every change.

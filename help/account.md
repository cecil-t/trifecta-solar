Your own sign-in and profile.

- **Profile:** your initials (up to 4 letters, shown in owner and assignee lists) and your phone (direct or cell; printed on proposals for salespeople). An admin changes your name and email.
- **Change password:** at least 8 characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol.
- **Signed-in devices:** each browser or phone stays signed in until it is signed out. Sign out one you no longer use, or **Sign out all other devices** if a phone is lost.

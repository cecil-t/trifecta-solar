One record per sold job: what each person is owed and what has been paid. Admins manage everything; a salesperson sees their own.

## Setting one up

From the project page's Contract card, **Set up the sales commission**. It fills in from the sold opportunity and build sheet (and links them to the project): commission, self-generated or referral bonus, other payouts (site visit, REAP paperwork) and any in-house handoff split.

## Status

- **Not due yet** until the project reaches Closeout, or, with that setting, until its final payment is in. **Waiting on final payment** means it's in Closeout with a payment step still open.
- **Owed**, **Part paid**, **Paid**, or **Paid in advance** (paid before it came due).

## Paying

- **Mark paid** on the commission, or completing the project's **Pay sales commission** step, pays every balance. Reopening that step takes it back.
- Payments and adjustments (with a reason) are recorded on the commission's page. A payment can be cash or covered by draws.
- **Referral bonuses to pay** lists bonuses owed to referrers, with their W-9 status.

## On this page

- **Draws against commission:** everyone on a draw, with their standing. Pick a person to open or set up their draw.
- **Commission settings** (admins): whether commission comes due at Closeout (PTO) or at Final payment received, and when a draw advance gets flagged.

Every system Trifecta monitors, across SolarEdge, Enphase, APsystems, Growatt and SMA, on one page.

- The server collects data from each manufacturer on a schedule; this page only shows what is stored. It reloads itself shortly after each quarter hour.
- **Tiles:** systems and how many have issues, installed size, yesterday, the last 7 and 30 days, and this year.
- **By manufacturer:** each brand's share of installed capacity and its average daily kWh per kW over the last 30 days, which shows a brand or site that lags.
- **Last 13 months:** production per month for the whole fleet.
- **Issues:** anything a manufacturer reports as an alert or offline, plus any system with no production on the last 3 complete days. **Open ticket** starts a service ticket filled in from the system; once a ticket is open, its number shows instead.
- SolarEdge is read on weekday mornings only (as often as its free API credits allow), so its weekend days fill in on Monday and it has no figure for today.
- Notes under the tiles and chart say when a figure is partial (a brand that hasn't reported yet, or history that starts partway through the year).

For an office screen, an admin makes a link under Lists > Display links.

The default list of steps copied into every new project. Admins only.

- Items are tasks with optional sub-tasks, grouped by phase: Pre-Install, Installation, Closeout and Payments, in their Order.
- **Applies to:** all projects, projects with solar PV, or projects with batteries.
- **Needed by default:** Yes, No, or **Ask** (the project shows a Yes / No / ? question).
- **Default owner** and **Reference # label** (permit #, work order # and so on).
- **Gate:** what completing it does to the project's status: Starts day counter, Required for Clear to install, Moves to Installation, or Moves to Closeout.
- **Customer update** (red dot): adds a line to the project's Notify customer card when the task is complete, the item is completed, a target date is set, or it's answered Yes. The line's wording is set here.

## What changes reach

Template changes apply to **new** projects only. Bring a step into an existing project with Add from template on its page. Customer update rules are the exception: they apply to existing projects from the moment they are set, without raising lines for what already happened.

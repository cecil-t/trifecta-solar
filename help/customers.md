The organizations and households Trifecta sells to and services. Anyone can add or edit a customer and its contacts.

- **The customer is the business or household**; the people are its contacts, each with a role, phone and email. A customer can have several contacts, and the proposal's "Addressed to" picks one.
- A customer's page lists its contacts, its projects and its activity.
- Customers are also added on the spot from New project, New opportunity and New service ticket.
- A customer with no projects yet shows as a **Prospect** on Sales.
- One customer can have many projects and opportunities (a second building, an expansion), so search for the existing customer before adding a new one.

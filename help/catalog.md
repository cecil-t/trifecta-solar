The internal cost of everything a build sheet can price. Everyone can view it; admins and salespeople edit it.

## An item

- **Category, name and notes.** Keep the name short; detail goes in Notes.
- **Cost to us** per unit, per watt or per foot (up to 4 decimals), plus shipping per unit, a per-line adder (trench start-up, tracker sensors) and a line minimum.
- **Tax:** whether sales tax applies (major equipment can go untaxed on tax-exempt jobs; balance of system never does).
- **Where it's offered:** Residential or Non-Residential, and which install types. Build sheets only offer items that fit.
- **Pass-through** (added after markup, no commission) and **pays salesperson** (site visit, REAP paperwork).
- **Specs and warranty:** watts, size (the site layout draws panels at this length and width), AC kW, battery kWh, the SAM (CEC) model for production, degradation, warranty years and wording, domestic content % and FEOC.

## Keeping prices current

- Every price carries its date and source. One older than the stale setting on Sales rates shows **Needs a price check**. **Confirm price** dates a price you checked without changing it.
- A new price never changes a saved build sheet. Those show "Catalog price changed" and can make a copy at today's prices.
- **Retire** an item instead of deleting it; old build sheets still name it.
- Trench builder parts (digging, boring, conduit, wire) are catalog items too.

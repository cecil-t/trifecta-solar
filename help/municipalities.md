Townships, boroughs and cities where Trifecta works. Each name is unique within its county. Anyone can add one.

- **Usual providers** (set by admins): who handles Zoning, the Building permit and Inspections in that municipality: the municipality itself, the county, or a third party from the Directory. New projects in that municipality start with these.
- Each municipality's page lists its contacts (admins edit) and its projects.
- A project finds its municipality from the site address (the Census lookup, with OpenStreetMap as a backup). When the lookup disagrees with the municipality picked on a project, the project's Jurisdiction card says so until someone settles it on the project's Edit page.

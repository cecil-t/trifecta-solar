Company-wide settings for build sheets and proposals. Everyone can read them; only admins change them.

New build sheets copy these in when they're created. A saved build sheet keeps its own copy, so a change here never changes a quote already made.

- **Pricing:** sales tax, default markup and its floor, rounding of the final price, default labor price and labor rate, lodging and per diem, how many days before a catalog price needs checking, and the angle where an array counts as steep.
- **Trench builder:** how the trench builder sizes wire and conduit.
- **Production estimate:** loss defaults for SAM and PVWatts, and module degradation when the catalog has none.
- **Price to Compare:** per utility, for the net metering true-up when the opportunity and OpenEI give none.
- **Savings:** rate increase, SRECs, analysis period, discount rate and depreciation defaults. A build sheet can change any of them for its own proposal.
- **Proposal:** company block, cover letter, next steps, valid-until default and limit, PA HIC number, and the insurance sentence with its end date.
- **Commission:** the commission and self-generated tiers (tax-bracket style), referral bonus and handoff share.
- **Contingency:** the rules, cap and minimum.
- **Deposits and Payment schedules:** deposit by job class and size, and the payment splits after it.
- **Warranty wording:** Trifecta and SolarInsure text for every warranty summary.
- **History:** every change, with who and when.

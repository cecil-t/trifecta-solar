A salesperson on a draw is paid a steady amount every payroll instead of each commission as it comes due. Admins manage it; a salesperson can view their own.

## The numbers

- **Advance** = draws paid less commission covered by draws. It rides on the person's **pipeline** (sold jobs not due yet).
- **Standing:** Ahead (with the advance as a share of pipeline; orange past the warning share, red past 100% or with no pipeline), Even, or Behind.

## Draw schedule

- Amount, first payroll and how often. A draw line is written for each payroll date as it arrives.
- **Change amount** from a payroll date on. **Stop after this payroll** ends it.
- A first payroll in the past writes the past draws too. To start from an opening balance instead, set the first payroll after it.

## Draws paid

- The newest show; older ones fold by year.
- Each payroll's line is automatic; Save only corrects one, and deleting one marks a skipped payroll. **Extra draw** adds a one-off.
- **Opening balance:** draws paid before the tracker that commission hasn't covered yet. One per person.
- Commission uses draws oldest first. A draw it has covered shows **Covered** and is frozen; changes have to come after the last covered draw.

## True-up

Applies the advance to due jobs, oldest first, and can record any catch-up still owed as cash. Jobs paid off close their project's "Pay sales commission" step. Mark paid and that step also take a draw rep's balance from the advance first.
